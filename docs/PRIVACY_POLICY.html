Privacy Policy for Vexel Finance Passport

Effective date: August 23, 2026

Vexel Finance Passport is a private, offline-first personal-finance organizer. This policy explains how the Android app handles information. The app is not a bank, payment service, government agency, tax authority, or tax adviser.

Summary

Vexel Finance Passport does not require an account and does not send your financial records, documents, or identifiers to us. The current app has no advertising, analytics, tracking, cloud sync, bank-login collection, payment initiation, or automatic tax filing.

Information you enter and store

You may choose to enter financial and organizational information such as accounts, transactions, income, expenses, assets, liabilities, goals, tax-preparation notes, and record details. You may also choose to import documents such as PDFs and images into the document vault.

This information is stored locally in the app on your device. Vault documents are stored in app-private encrypted storage. Certain sensitive values may also be protected using Android Keystore-backed encryption. The app does not collect biometric templates; device biometric authentication is handled by Android and only returns an authentication result to the app.

How information is used

Your information is used only on your device to provide the features you choose, including record keeping, calculations, reminders, reports, tax-preparation organization, document storage, and backup/restore. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or sale.

Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell, rent, share, or disclose your financial data to third parties because the app does not transmit it to us. Information leaves your device only when you deliberately use an app feature to export a report or data file, create a backup, or select a file destination. Once you share an exported file or backup with another person or service, its handling is governed by that recipient's practices.

Backups and exports

Android automatic cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled for app data. If you create a portable backup, the app encrypts it with a password you provide. You are responsible for choosing a secure destination and protecting that password. Do not lose the password: the app may be unable to restore the backup without it. Reports and exports may contain financial information; please store and share them carefully.

Permissions

  • Notifications: if you allow notification permission, the app can show local reminders that you configure, including document-expiry or financial reminders. You can turn this off in Android settings.
  • User-selected files: when you choose to import a document, export data, or save/restore a backup, Android's system file picker gives the app access only to the file or location you select. The app does not request broad storage access.
  • Biometric/device security: where supported and chosen by you, Android's biometric or device-credential prompt can unlock the app. Biometric data remains managed by your device and is not stored or received by Vexel Finance Passport.

Retention and deletion

Your records remain on your device until you delete individual records or use the app's delete-all-data control, uninstall the app, or remove a backup/export yourself. Deleting data in the app does not delete copies you previously exported or backed up to a location you control.

Security

The app uses app-private storage, encrypted document storage, and Android security features to help protect local data. No method of storage is perfectly secure, so protect your device, screen lock, PIN, backup password, and exported files.

Children

The app is not directed to children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children because it does not transmit user-entered information to us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app's features or data practices change. Any update will be published at the privacy-policy link associated with the app before or when the relevant version is released.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the developer contact information shown on the app's Google Play Store listing. Before publishing, ensure that the Play Console developer contact email is monitored.
